Our story

We built the guide we wished we had.

LostPhones began after a phone went missing in our own family. We saw how quickly the situation became overwhelming: which account should be secured first, whether to lock or erase the device, who to contact, and which advice could be trusted.

Useful information existed, but it was scattered across carrier pages, device-maker support articles, and general checklists. We created LostPhones to bring the essential actions together in a calmer, easier order.

What to expect

Helpful guidance, with honest limits.

LostPhones cannot track, unlock, remotely control, or recover a device for you. We do not have access to your phone, Apple Account, Google Account, carrier account, location, passwords, or verification codes.

Results depend on your device, settings, battery and network state, and the services available from Apple, Google, your carrier, law enforcement, or your insurer. We cannot guarantee that a phone will be found or that data can be recovered.

Our content is general educational information, not legal, financial, insurance, cybersecurity, or emergency advice. If theft or personal safety is involved, do not confront anyone; contact local law enforcement or emergency services as appropriate.
