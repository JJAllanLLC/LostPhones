A useful clue, offered free

Why check an IMEI?

When you are identifying or considering a phone, its IMEI prefix can offer another clue about the device model described to you. LostPhones provides this limited model check free for visitors, without an account or payment.

01

Find the number

Copy the 15-digit IMEI shown in the phone's settings, on its packaging, or by the device's own IMEI display method.

02

Run the local check

Enter all 15 digits. This page compares the first eight digits with a small built-in list of recognized device prefixes.

03

Treat it as a clue

A recognized model can help you compare basic device information, but this result does not verify the seller, ownership, condition, eligibility, or whether a phone is safe to buy.
