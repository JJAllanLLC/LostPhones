Lost Phone in Uber or Lyft? Exact Recovery Steps

Published January 2025 · Updated October 6, 2026

By JJ Allan

Take a deep breath. You're in the right place.

Losing your phone in a rideshare is one of the most common ways people lose devices — and one of the easiest to recover if you act fast. This is the exact process I used when I left mine in an Uber — I got it back the same day.

Quick Printable Checklist (Do These First)

  • ☐ 1. Open the Uber or Lyft app → Trips → select the ride
  • ☐ 2. Tap "Lost Item" and follow the prompts
  • ☐ 3. Use the in-app message to contact the driver immediately
  • ☐ 4. Check Find My / Find Hub while waiting
  • ☐ 5. If no response in 10-15 minutes, call the driver through the app
  • ☐ 6. Offer a small reward ($20-$50) — it works
  • ☐ 7. If still no luck, file a support ticket with Uber/Lyft
  • ☐ 8. Follow the full recovery checklist while waiting

Full Step-by-Step Guide with Contact Scripts

Step 1: Use the In-App Lost Item Feature Immediately

Both Uber and Lyft have a dedicated "Lost Item" flow — this is your fastest path.

Uber:

  • Open the Uber app → Trips → select the ride
  • Scroll down → "I lost an item"
  • Choose "Contact driver about a lost item"
  • Enter your phone number and a short message

Lyft:

  • Open the Lyft app → Menu → Ride history → select the ride
  • Tap "Get help" → "I lost an item"
  • Fill out the form and submit

Step 2: Message the Driver (Exact Script That Works)

Most drivers respond within minutes if you use this calm, polite script:

"Hi [Driver Name], I believe I left my [color] iPhone/Android in your car after my ride today. I'm happy to offer a $30 reward for its safe return. Please let me know a good time/place to meet or if you can drop it at an Uber/Lyft hub. Thank you so much!"

Drivers are much more likely to help when a reward is offered upfront.

Step 3: Call the Driver (If Available)

Uber and Lyft mask both numbers, so the call is anonymous. Use it — drivers often answer calls faster than messages.

Step 4: Track While Waiting

Use Find My or Find Hub — many phones are recovered because the driver sees the location and realises it's in the car.

Step 5: If No Response After 30 Minutes

File a formal support ticket:

  • Uber: help.uber.com → Lost Item
  • Lyft: help.lyft.com → Contact Us → Lost Item

What Usually Happens

Use the rideshare company’s official lost-item process promptly and give the driver a safe, simple way to return the phone. Recovery cannot be guaranteed.
