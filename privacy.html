Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains the information handled when you use LostPhones.com and the services that help us operate the site.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Send by Email

Our Contact page opens your email application; LostPhones does not operate a website contact form. If you email us, we receive the address, message, and other information you choose to include and use it to respond to your inquiry.

B. Hosting and Usage Data

LostPhones is hosted on Vercel. Vercel and the analytics services described below may process request and usage information such as IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, approximate visit timing, and referring page according to their own services and policies.

C. Analytics and Browser Storage

On lostphones.com, we use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and Vercel Analytics to understand site use and performance. LostPhones limits its custom analytics events to fixed categories and does not include passwords, verification codes, email addresses, phone numbers, IMEIs, session tokens, full URLs, or free-form incident descriptions in those event fields.

The site also uses browser storage for limited functions such as recovery-session tokens on a trusted device, analytics workflow markers, and remembering whether certain prompts have already appeared. You can clear site data through your browser settings.

D. Optional Guided Recovery Resume

The recovery guide can optionally store a recovery session for up to seven days so you can return to the same plan. That record contains only bounded multiple-choice recovery state, schema/flow versions, and timestamps. It does not include passwords, verification codes, email addresses, phone numbers, IMEI or serial numbers, precise locations, report numbers, financial details, or free-form incident descriptions.

The resume link uses an opaque token kept in the browser address fragment or, on a trusted device you choose, in local storage. The server stores only a SHA-256 hash of that token. Public or shared devices do not save a resume token. Starting over deletes the server record when one exists. Resume tokens are never sent to analytics.

E. Free IMEI Model Check

The free IMEI model check runs in your browser. LostPhones does not offer a paid IMEI report and the free check does not send the IMEI to a LostPhones checkout or IMEI-report provider.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to deliver site functions, optionally resume a recovery plan, verify an eligible paid recovery-plan purchase, provide the purchased download, respond to email, understand site use, and maintain site performance and security.

3. Third-Party Services

Current service providers include:

  • Vercel – website hosting and site analytics
  • Upstash – optional recovery-session and paid recovery-plan purchase context
  • Stripe – payment processing for the $8.95 recovery-plan download (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Google Analytics – site usage measurement (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Microsoft Clarity – session replay and interaction measurement (privacy.microsoft.com)
  • Amazon and other linked sites – only when you choose to follow an external or affiliate link

4. Storage, Security, and Retention

Optional guided-recovery records and the context used to verify a paid recovery-plan purchase are configured to expire after seven days. Starting Over deletes an available guided-recovery record. Payment-card information is entered through Stripe Checkout rather than a LostPhones payment form. Other providers retain information according to their own services and policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

You can use the free recovery, preparedness, blog, and IMEI-model guidance without creating a LostPhones account. Saving a recovery session is optional. You can use Start Over to remove an available saved recovery session and clear LostPhones browser data through your browser settings. Requests about information sent directly to LostPhones can be emailed to the address below; information controlled by a service provider is also subject to that provider's request process.

6. Children's Privacy

LostPhones does not require account registration or ask users to provide their age. The guided recovery flow is designed not to request names, contact details, passwords, verification codes, IMEIs, or free-form incident descriptions. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information directly to LostPhones by email may contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

8. Contact

LostPhones.com is operated by JJ ALLAN, LLC. Privacy questions may be sent to lostphones@jjallanllc.com.
